A living person

A living personA living person is not just someone who physically exists, but someone who lives consciously, feels the fullness of life, and finds harmony with themselves, the world around them, and their highest values. A living person is capable of experiencing, creating, loving, learning, and inspiring others with their sincerity and inner light.

Signs of a living person#

Mindfulness

A living person is aware of his thoughts, feelings and actions, and strives to be in harmony with himself and his environment.

Sensitivity

He is open to emotions and impressions, knows how to experience both joy and pain, accepting it as a part of life.

Sincerity

A living person does not hide behind masks, he is honest with himself and others, and lives in accordance with the truth.

Harmony with nature

He feels his connection with nature, its rhythms and laws, respects and protects it.

Creativity and development

A living person constantly learns, develops, creates something new and shares it with the world.

Energy and inspiration

He is filled with energy, a desire for life, for moving forward, for searching for new meanings.

Connection with spirituality

A living person feels his place in the world and his connection with something greater - with the Universe, God, or higher values.

What makes a person alive?#

The ability to feel

A living person feels everything that happens: joy, pain, love, inspiration. This is the ability to be authentic and open to the world.

The ability to live in the present

He doesn't get stuck in the past or dwell on the future, but lives here and now, enjoying every moment.

The desire to create

A living person strives to leave behind something valuable, be it art, ideas or good deeds.

Openness to the world

He is open to new things, to other people, to changes and opportunities that life offers.

The energy of kindness

A living person cares for others, shares warmth and support.

A living person in the context of the TREE#

Sense of community

In the TREE community, a living person is revealed through interaction, support from other participants, and the pursuit of common goals.

Natural connection

A living person feels unity with nature, participates in its preservation and is inspired by its harmony.

Creative energy

At TREE, living people create through art, crafts, music, and innovation, leaving their mark on culture and society.

Conscious interaction

A living person builds sincere and deep relationships based on respect and empathy.

Spiritual development

Meditation, mindfulness, and trust circle practices help a person to be alive not only physically, but also spiritually.

How to become a living person?#

Listen to yourself

Notice your feelings, desires, and aspirations. Live in accordance with them.

Be open to new things

Try new ideas, travel, learn, expand your horizons.

Be sincere

Speak and act honestly, in harmony with your values.

Find joy in the little things

Learn to see the beauty in everyday life: in a smile, in the smell of nature, in music.

Share your energy

Help others, inspire them with your example.

Develop a connection with nature

Spending time in nature, feeling its rhythms, you become filled with vital energy.

Be creative

Create something that reflects your personality and inspires others.

Work on spiritual growth

Practice meditation, mindfulness, develop inner harmony.

How does a living person differ from someone who “just exists”?#

A living person

Feels, creates, inspires, searches for meaning, acts with love and awareness.

Just existing

Lives on autopilot, avoids feelings, avoids change, acts out of fear or routine.

A living person— is someone who sees meaning in every moment, strives for harmony with themselves and the world, and gives their energy and inspiration to others.

How do you feel alive? What fills your life with vital energy?
