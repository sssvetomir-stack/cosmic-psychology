Cosmic faith#

Cosmic faith— is a philosophical and spiritual worldview based on the idea of ​​man as part of a single Universe, where nature, society and the cosmos form an interconnected system.

Its goal is to develop a mature personality, strengthen mental and physical health, foster responsibility for future generations, preserve nature, and foster a culture of harmonious coexistence between humans and the surrounding world.

Cosmic faith does not oppose science or seek to replace existing religions. It views scientific knowledge, philosophy, cultural traditions, and personal spiritual experience as different ways of understanding the world.

02

Basic principles#

Unity of life#

All forms of life exist within a common system of interconnections. Humans are part of nature, and nature is part of the broader processes of the universe.

Any human action has an impact on the surrounding world.

Responsibility#

Freedom is impossible without responsibility.

Liability extends to:

  • own health;
  • family;
  • society;
  • nature;
  • future generations.

Knowledge#

Learning about the world never ends.

The study of man requires the integration of various fields of knowledge:

  • psychology;
  • biology;
  • medicine;
  • ecology;
  • anthropology;
  • astronomy;
  • philosophy;
  • history of culture.

Harmony#

Harmony is understood as a search for a reasonable balance between:

  • mind;
  • feelings;
  • body;
  • society;
  • nature;
  • technologies.

Development#

Every person is capable of constant development.

Development includes:

  • education;
  • creativity;
  • work;
  • service to society;
  • internal improvement.

Cosmic psychology#

Cosmic psychology is a developmentNew Psychology DREVO.

While New Psychology DREVO studies man as a single living system, Cosmic Psychology views him in a broader context:

  • collective behavior of society.

It explores the influence of the environment on a person's mental state and examines various scientific and philosophical models of human interaction with the outside world.

Science and research#

Cosmic faith supports free exploration of the world.

She encourages:

  • scientific method;
  • critical thinking;
  • hypothesis testing;
  • openness to new knowledge;
  • respect for different philosophical views.

Issues on which there is no scientific consensus (for example, certain ideas about the aura, energetic interactions, or the influence of astronomical factors on the psyche) may be studied as a matter of philosophical, cultural, or research interest, but are not considered established scientific fact without appropriate evidence.

Servants of the cosmic faith#

The primary task of the servants of the cosmic faith is to promote the preservation, development, and strengthening of human mental health. Their mission is to help people develop the capacity for mindfulness, emotional stability, critical thinking, moral growth, and harmonious relationships with themselves, society, nature, and the Universe.

AboutPrimary responsibilities may include:

  • Caring for a person's mental and emotional well-being.
  • Support for personal and intellectual development.
  • Help in finding the meaning of life and inner harmony.
  • Resolving conflicts peacefully and ethically.
  • Education in science, philosophy and culture.
  • Formation of a respectful attitude towards nature, life and future generations.
  • Supporting communities where people can grow, collaborate, and help each other.

To understand a person's individual characteristics, practitioners of the cosmic faith strive to compile the most complete picture possible of their personality and health. With the individual's voluntary consent, their genealogy and family history, lifestyle, medical examination results, extensive blood tests, and physical and mental health are studied. If the individual so desires, traditional cultural practices of self-knowledge may also be considered, such as an astrological birth chart, iris analysis (iridology), and the characteristics of the palms and, in some cases, the feet. These methods are not used as standalone medical diagnostic tools, but are considered elements of a person's personal worldview and do not replace scientifically based diagnosis and treatment.

Servants of the cosmic faith engage in a comprehensive study of humans and their interactions with the surrounding world. Their work includes research into consciousness, psychology, social connections, and collective intelligence, the influence of natural and astronomical phenomena on humans, and the study of traditions, rituals, and the cultural heritage of various peoples.

Within the spiritual-philosophical framework, they also explore concepts of the aura, human field interaction, and other models proposed by various philosophical and esoteric traditions. These approaches are viewed as a subject of ideological and research interest and do not replace conclusions based on modern science and medicine.

The main goal of such research is a better understanding of human nature, strengthening mental and physical health, personal development, preserving cultural heritage, and finding ways to harmoniously coexist between humans, society, nature, and the Universe.

Research activities of the servants of the Cosmic Faith DREVO#

The servants of the Cosmic Faith DREVO dedicate their work to the comprehensive study of humanity, nature, society, and their interconnections. Their goal is to seek a deeper understanding of the laws of life that foster personal development, improved health, the preservation of culture, and harmonious interaction between humans and the surrounding world.

Research activities cover a wide range of areas, including:

  • the nature of consciousness, thinking and personality;
  • human psychology and the patterns of its development;
  • the influence of family, clan, culture and society on the formation of personality;
  • mechanisms for cooperation, collective intelligence and community development;
  • human interaction with the natural environment;
  • the influence of natural, climatic, geophysical and astronomical phenomena on human life;
  • the history of civilizations, world cultures, traditions and spiritual heritage of peoples;
  • issues of ecology, sustainable development and responsible attitude to the environment;
  • the relationship between science, philosophy, culture and technology.

Within the framework of philosophical and ideological research, various historical and modern ideas about man can also be considered, including the concepts of the soul, aura, field interaction, vital energy and other models found in the philosophical, religious and esoteric traditions of different cultures.

Such concepts are studied as part of humanity's cultural and intellectual heritage, as well as a subject of philosophical analysis. Their consideration does not constitute acceptance of their scientific validity and does not replace the results of research based on modern scientific methods.

The DREVO Cosmic Faith recognizes the value of scientific knowledge and regards science as one of the most important ways of understanding the world. It also acknowledges the importance of philosophy, culture, history, and personal spiritual experience as independent sources of understanding human life. Each of these fields has its own research methods, applicability limits, and evaluation criteria.

The main goal of research activities is:

  • deepening understanding of human nature;
  • strengthening physical, mental and social well-being;
  • development of a mature, responsible and creative personality;
  • preservation and study of the cultural heritage of humanity;
  • development of dialogue between science, philosophy and culture;
  • the search for ways of harmonious coexistence between man, society, nature and the Universe;
  • transfer of accumulated knowledge to future generations.

The fundamental principle of the DREVO Cosmic Faith is openness to knowledge. Any hypothesis, idea, or tradition can be the subject of research if it is examined honestly, critically, with respect for humanity, and with an understanding of the differences between scientific knowledge, philosophical reflection, and spiritual experience.

Truth is born from the desire to understand, not from the quest to confirm one's own beliefs at any cost. Therefore, the servants of the DREVO Cosmic Faith maintain an openness to new knowledge, respect for the scientific method, attention to cultural heritage, and responsibility to humanity, society, and future generations.

Cosmic Faith DREVO#

Cosmic Faith— is a philosophical and spiritual worldview based on the recognition of humanity as part of a unified universe. It expresses the desire to live in harmony with nature, society, and the laws of the universe, constantly expanding one's knowledge of oneself and the world around one.

Cosmic Faith embraces respect for scientific knowledge, philosophical inquiry, spiritual experience, and the cultural heritage of humanity. It does not require the renunciation of existing religious or non-religious beliefs, but rather offers a common language of responsibility, creativity, and the pursuit of truth.

Main ideas#

The Universe as a single system#

The universe is viewed as an interconnected system of which humans, nature, and society are all part. Understanding these interconnections helps us make more responsible decisions and see the consequences of our actions.

Man as a part of the cosmos#

Every person is part of nature and simultaneously a participant in broader processes occurring on Earth and in the Universe. This awareness fosters a sense of responsibility for one's own life and the world around us.

Continuous learning#

Cosmic Faith recognizes that human knowledge is constantly evolving. Therefore, it encourages exploration, open dialogue, education, and a willingness to reconsider one's beliefs as new data emerges.

Harmony#

Harmony is understood as a search for balance between man and nature, the individual and society, technology and ecology, freedom and responsibility.

Creation#

The main value is considered to be an activity that benefits people, nature and future generations.

Cosmic Faith and DREVO#

In the DREVO system, Cosmic Faith serves as a worldview foundation that unites various areas of activity.

She inspires development:

  • New Psychology DREVO;
  • ecological thinking;
  • science and education;
  • technologies that work in harmony with nature;
  • culture of cooperation;
  • healthy lifestyle;
  • long-term thinking and responsibility to future generations.

Practice#

The practical expression of Cosmic Faith lies not only in reflection but also in daily actions.

It manifests itself through:

  • taking care of one's own physical and mental health;
  • respectful attitude towards people;
  • conservation of nature;
  • observation of natural cycles;
  • study of the universe and scientific discoveries;
  • creativity;
  • Gratitude;
  • voluntary service to society;
  • development of critical thinking and the ability to self-analyze.

Some followers may also use meditative practices, symbolic rituals, or stargazing as means of personal spiritual experience. These practices are voluntary and reflect a person's individual path.

Attitude to science#

Cosmic Faith views science as one of the most important ways to understand the world. It supports the exploration of nature, space, and humanity, respects the scientific method, and is open to new discoveries.

Philosophical and spiritual ideas that have not yet received scientific confirmation are considered as worldview models and a subject for free research, and not as established knowledge.

Mission#

The mission of the Cosmic Faith is to foster a society in which knowledge, spirituality, culture, technology, and nature are not opposed to one another, but rather complement each other for the sake of human development and the preservation of life on Earth.

Motto#

"By understanding ourselves, we better understand humanity. By understanding humanity, we better understand nature. By understanding nature, we more deeply understand the universe. And by understanding the universe, we rediscover ourselves."

Three foundations of the DREVO worldview#

I. Cosmic Faith#

Worldview and philosophy#

Cosmic Faith is the highest level of DREVO's worldview. It answers questions about humanity's place in the Universe, the meaning of life, and responsibility to nature, society, and future generations.

It forms a system of values ​​based on:

  • the unity of man, nature and the Universe;
  • respect for life;
  • search for truth;
  • harmony;
  • freedom and responsibility;
  • creation;
  • continuous knowledge.

Cosmic Faith unites philosophy, spiritual experience, cultural heritage and scientific knowledge as different ways of exploring reality.

II. New Psychology of DREVO#

Science and philosophy of man#

The new psychology of DREVO is a scientific and philosophical model of man.

She views a person as a single living system that unites:

  • body;
  • psyche;
  • consciousness;
  • heredity;
  • social environment;
  • culture;
  • interaction with nature.

It is developed on its basisCosmic psychology, which expands human exploration by considering the influence of broader natural, geophysical, social, and cosmic processes. It can examine both accepted scientific data and philosophical hypotheses, while distinguishing between the levels of evidence.

III. Family Traditions#

Tradition, culture and social structure#

"Family Traditions" is the cultural basis of DREVO.

It preserves and develops:

  • ancestral memory;
  • traditions;
  • rituals;
  • brotherhood;
  • family values;
  • mutual assistance;
  • patrimonial law;
  • cooperation;
  • transfer of knowledge between generations.

Particular attention is paid to the voluntary unification of Clans, the principles of honesty, trust, mutual support, the organization of joint projects, cooperatives, educational centers, and rituals symbolizing the acceptance of responsibility to the Clan and the community.
