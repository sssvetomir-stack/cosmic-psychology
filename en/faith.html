Why is it called Faith?#

Name "Cosmic Faith DREVO"was chosen consciously. It reflects the nature of this system, its boundaries, and its attitude toward knowledge.

Cosmic Faith is not an exact science because it includes not only verified facts, measurable phenomena, and reproducible experiments. It also draws on philosophy, spiritual experience, cultural traditions, historical observations, symbolic systems, intuitive knowledge, and hypotheses about the laws of human life.

At the same time, it is not a dogmatic religion, since it does not demand unconditional acceptance of a single teaching, does not declare its ideas to be the final truth, and does not forbid a person to doubt, examine, and reconsider his own views.

That is why the most precise concept becomes the word"Faith".

What does Faith mean?#

In this context, faith is not a blind acceptance of claims without evidence.

Faith is understood as an inner trust in certain values, principles, and the direction of human development. It is a conscious worldview choice that helps a person answer questions that go beyond the capabilities of precise measurement:

  • what is the meaning of human life;
  • what is the place of man in nature and the Universe;
  • is there a responsibility to future generations;
  • what a worthy life path should be;
  • how are personality, family, clan, society and nature connected;
  • what is soul, conscience, love, freedom and purpose;
  • How should a person relate to the unknown?

Science is capable of exploring many aspects of humanity and the world around us, but it cannot always provide definitive answers to questions of meaning, moral choice, and personal purpose. These questions belong to the realm of worldview, philosophy, and faith.

Why not "exact science"#

Exact science is based on measurements, testable hypotheses, reproducible experiments, and the ability to disprove a false statement.

Cosmic Psychology and Cosmic Faith utilize scientific knowledge, but are not limited to it. They also consider:

  • philosophical ideas about man;
  • centuries-old traditions of different peoples;
  • models of the soul, consciousness, aura and biofield;
  • astrological systems;
  • symbols and archetypes;
  • individual spiritual experience;
  • observations of recurring patterns in life;
  • assumptions about the influence of natural and cosmic cycles;
  • forecasts that cannot be absolutely accurate.

Some of these ideas can be scientifically investigated. Others lack sufficient evidence or, by their very nature, are purely philosophical and subjective.

Therefore, it would be incorrect to call the entire system a science. This would create the false impression that every proposition has already been experimentally proven.

Word "Faith"honestly shows that the system contains not only knowledge, but also understanding, assumption, hope, trust and freely chosen values.

Why not just "philosophy"#

Philosophy helps us reflect on the nature of the world, humanity, consciousness, truth, and morality. It is an important part of the Cosmic Faith, but it does not exhaust it.

Cosmic Faith presupposes not only reflection but also a life stance. It must be manifested in a person's actions:

  • in respect for life;
  • in caring for the family;
  • in supporting talents;
  • in creative work;
  • in nature conservation;
  • in responsibility to society;
  • in the development of knowledge;
  • in helping other people;
  • in passing on experience to future generations.

Philosophy can be studied abstractly, without changing one's life. Faith, however, becomes an internal guide that influences decisions, relationships, and behavior.

Therefore, Cosmic Faith is not only a system of thoughts, but also a way of life.

Why not "ideology"#

Ideology is often created to organize society around a specific political, economic, or social model. It can demand unity of views and be used in the struggle for power.

The DREVO Cosmic Faith should not be turned into an instrument of political control. It does not dictate which party a person should vote for, which economic theory they should adhere to, or which social group they should belong to.

Its basis is universal human values:

  • life;
  • dignity;
  • freedom;
  • responsibility;
  • family;
  • creation;
  • knowledge;
  • mutual assistance;
  • health;
  • nature;
  • development;
  • peaceful cooperation.

A person can belong to different cultures, peoples, states and religious traditions, while maintaining the right to share the values ​​of the Cosmic Faith.

Why not an ordinary religion?#

Cosmic Faith can perform some of the functions of religion: helping a person find meaning, form moral guidelines, unite people, and maintain connections between generations.

However, it is not based on the requirement to accept a single, obligatory worldview. It should not declare other beliefs false, and it does not presuppose persecution for doubting.

Cosmic Faith recognizes that humanity has created many spiritual traditions. Each contains historical experience, symbols, moral ideas, and ways of understanding life.

DREVO's mission is not to destroy these traditions, but to study their experiences, find unifying values, and create space for dialogue.

A person may maintain their religious affiliation or belong to no religion. Participation in the Cosmic Faith must remain voluntary.

Faith as a space between knowledge and the unknown#

Humanity always lives on the border between the known and the unknown.

What was once the subject of philosophical speculation could later become the object of scientific research. At the same time, each new discovery raises new questions.

Cosmic Faith exists precisely in this space. It respects proven knowledge, but recognizes that our current understanding of the world is not definitive.

It allows a person to:

  • use the achievements of science;
  • maintain a philosophical openness;
  • study cultural heritage;
  • formulate new hypotheses;
  • look for patterns;
  • acknowledge uncertainty;
  • do not pass off an assumption as a proven fact;
  • maintain hope and direction of development.

Thus, faith is not opposed to knowledge. It begins when a person honestly acknowledges the limits of available evidence but continues to seek meaning and build a responsible life.

Faith does not cancel out doubt#

In Cosmic Faith, doubt is not seen as a weakness or a violation.

Reasonable doubt protects a person from delusions, manipulation, and fanaticism. It encourages one to verify information, compare sources, examine consequences, and reconsider erroneous conclusions.

Therefore, Cosmic Faith unites two states:

faith as an internal direction And doubt as a tool of verification.

A person can believe in the value of life, development, love, justice, and the unity of nature, while critically evaluating specific predictions, methods, and explanations.

Attitude to forecasts#

Cosmic Faith studies patterns, cycles, and possible connections. These patterns can be used to make predictions and recommendations.

However, the forecast should not be passed off as an inevitable fate.

Any forecast reflects only a possible development based on available data, observations, the chosen model, and its limitations. People retain freedom of choice, and circumstances are subject to change.

Astrological, symbolic, and intuitive forecasts should be used with particular caution. They can serve as food for thought, but should not become the basis for:

  • establishing a medical diagnosis;
  • refusal of treatment;
  • restrictions on human rights;
  • determining its value;
  • forced marriage or divorce;
  • choosing a profession against a person’s will;
  • financial and legal decisions;
  • declaring a person dangerous, damned, or doomed.
  • Faith should help a person become freer and more responsible, and not more dependent on predictions.

Faith as trust in development#

One of the main foundations of Cosmic Faith is trust in the possibility of development.

She argues that a person is not reducible to their mistakes, background, diagnoses, social status, or past experiences. They possess within them abilities that can be developed through education, support, hard work, relationships, creativity, and self-discovery.

Therefore, the Cosmic Faith pays special attention to:

  • identification of talents;
  • accompanying the individual;
  • raising children;
  • family support;
  • transfer of knowledge;
  • strengthening health;
  • creating conditions for self-realization;
  • serving society and nature.

To believe in a person does not mean to idealize him, but to recognize his ability to learn, change, take responsibility and create something new.

Responsible Faith#

Cosmic Faith should not be a justification for arbitrary statements.

The phrase "I believe" doesn't transform a proposition into an objective fact. Therefore, the status of each proposition within the system must be clearly defined:

  • proven fact;
  • scientific hypothesis;
  • observed pattern;
  • philosophical interpretation;
  • cultural tradition;
  • symbolic model;
  • personal spiritual experience;
  • forecast or assumption.

This distinction protects the Cosmic Faith from dogmatism and pseudoscience. It allows for respect for spiritual experience without replacing it with medicine, psychology, astronomy, and other sciences.

Why is the word written with a capital letter?#

In common speech, faith means personal trust or belief. In the title"Space Faith DREVO"The word can be written with a capital letter, since it denotes an independent worldview system.

It unites:

  • idea of ​​a person;
  • value system;
  • attitude towards nature and the Universe;
  • spiritual and cultural traditions;
  • practice of self-knowledge;
  • moral responsibility;
  • striving for development;
  • service to life and future generations.

The capital letter denotes the name of the direction, but does not mean superiority over other religions, philosophies or worldviews.

Final definition#

The DREVO Cosmic Faith is called Faith because it combines scientific knowledge with philosophical understanding, cultural traditions, spiritual experience, the study of patterns and cautious forecasts, without presenting the entirety of its ideas as exact science.

It is Faith because it offers a person not only information about the world, but also a value-based direction for life.

It is Cosmic because it considers a person as a part of a family, clan, society, nature, Earth and the Universe.

It is a DREVO system because it understands development as the growth of a tree: from the roots of heredity and culture, through the trunk of the individual and society, to the branches of knowledge, creativity, the future, and new generations.

Science helps people understand how the world works. Philosophy helps them make sense of it. Faith helps them determine why they live and how they should use the knowledge they gain.
