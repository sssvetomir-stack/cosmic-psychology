Emotions

EmotionsEmotions are immediate psychophysiological reactions to events that reflect our attitude toward them. They are closely related to feelings, but are distinguished by their transience and greater intensity. Emotions serve as an important tool for understanding the world, communicating, and making decisions.

What is the difference between emotions and feelings?#

Temporariness

Emotions are fleeting and arise in response to a specific stimulus (for example, joy from a gift or anger from injustice).

Feelings are a deeper and more lasting state (for example, love, friendship, affection).

Intensity

Emotions are stronger and more often expressed at the physiological level (heartbeat, sweating, facial expressions).

Feelings are calmer and more conscious.

Reactivity

Emotions often arise automatically, without thinking.

Feelings take time to form and analyze.

Basic types of emotions#

Positive

Joy, delight, satisfaction, gratitude, inspiration.

These emotions strengthen mental health and motivate us to take action.

Negative

Fear, anger, sadness, disgust, envy.

They are often perceived as negative, but they play an important role in signaling problems or dangers.

Mixed emotions

Ambivalence (for example, feeling both happy and sad at the same time) often arises in complex situations that require deep reflection.

The role of emotions in life#

A tool for self-knowledge

Emotions reflect our needs, desires, and values. For example, anger can indicate a violation of boundaries, while joy can indicate movement in the right direction.

means of communication

Emotions help people understand each other even without words: facial expressions, gestures, and tone of voice say a lot.

Motivation

Emotions motivate us to take action: fear can make us avoid danger, while inspiration can make us create something new.

Signals of change

Emotions serve as indicators, showing that something in our lives requires attention or change.

Emotions in the context of TREE#

Conscious perception of emotions

In TREE practices, participants learn to notice their emotions, understand their causes, and channel them into a constructive direction.

Expression of emotions

Through theatre, improvisation, music and creativity, DREVO participants find safe ways to express their emotions.

Emotional harmony

Community interactions are based on respect and understanding of each other's emotions. This helps create an atmosphere of trust and mutual support.

Nature and emotions

Interacting with nature helps calm negative emotions, strengthen positive ones, and find emotional balance.

How to work with emotions?#

Acceptance

Don't suppress your emotions. Recognize them as a natural reaction to what's happening.

Mindfulness

Ask yourself questions: "What am I feeling right now?", "Why am I reacting this way?", "What do I want to achieve?"

Expression of emotions

Find a healthy way to express your emotions: through conversation, creativity, movement, or breathing practices.

Regulation

Learn to manage your emotions so they don't take over your actions. For example, meditation or deep breathing practices can help calm strong emotions.

Empathy training

Learn to understand other people's emotions to strengthen relationships and find harmony in communication.

Why is it important to be aware of emotions?#

Emotional health

Understanding and managing emotions helps avoid stress, anxiety, and burnout.

Harmony in relationships

Understanding emotions improves communication and helps avoid conflicts.

Personal growth

Working with emotions helps you better understand yourself, develop emotional intelligence, and achieve your goals.

Connection with the inner world

Emotions are the bridge between the mind and the Soul, and their awareness helps to find balance and harmony.

Emotions— are a valuable tool that allows a person to better understand themselves and the world around them. They can be a source of energy, inspiration, and growth if we learn to recognize and channel them.
