God in the concept of TREE

God in conceptTREE— a vibrant, multifaceted idea that intertwines with the project's core principles and values. It's an understanding of God as a source of inspiration, harmony, energy, culture, and unification. Let's consider how this concept is revealed through the key aspects of TREE:

1. God as the source of life and energy#

In the TREE, God manifests through practices that help one experience life as a flow of energy.

Breathing and movement practices allow you to connect with the divine flow within yourself.

It is the realization that life is a gift that must be cherished, developed and passed on.

2. God as a collective consciousness#

Within the framework of this idea, God is seen as a collective mind that unites people for the sake of joint creation.

It is an emphasis on interaction, cooperation, support and the awareness that each person is part of a whole, larger tree of life.

God here is the force that inspires people to unite for a higher purpose.

3. God through culture#

In creative circles, God manifests as inspiration.

Art, poetry, theatre, music and self-expression reveal the bright sides of the human soul, bringing us closer to the highest.

Every creative act is an opportunity to touch the divine through the process of creation.

4. God as an ecological balance#

Nature and the surrounding world are the embodiment of the divine. Respect for the land, support for farmers, and responsible consumption of food are all forms of honoring God in nature.

The divine manifests through ensuring that every person has access to quality, healthy, and ethically produced food.

The ecological approach is respect for the harmony established by God in the world.

5. God as Inner Light and Personal Transformation#

Phelp participants develop themselves, find inner balance and reveal their potential.

God here is the inner light that helps a person follow his own path, free himself from fears and find the strength for change.

Through these practices, each person finds their own personal path to the divine.

6. God as a synthesis of directions#

God unites all key areas: culture, technology, health, cohabitation and production.

This is the idea that every area of ​​life is connected to the divine, and only the integration of all these aspects allows one to experience the fullness of being.

God in the TREE— is not a dogma or an object of worship, but a living idea that inspires, unites, and helps people realize that they are part of something bigger.

Which of these aspects do you find most important? Or would you like to add something of your own to the concept?
