Inspiration

InspirationInspiration is a special state when a person experiences a surge of energy, ideas, and the desire to create or act. It's a subtle moment when a connection with something greater emerges within: with one's inner potential, a higher power, nature, or the surrounding world. Inspiration awakens creativity, motivation, and helps one see life from a new perspective.

What is inspiration?#

The energy of creation

It is a feeling that fills us with energy to create something new - from a work of art to solving life's problems.

Connection with the depths

Inspiration is often experienced as a "breath" that connects us to our inner world or higher source.

Impulse for action

This is the moment when ideas or desires become so strong that we feel the need to make them come true.

A moment of clarity

During inspiration, a person sees solutions or paths that were previously unnoticed.

Emotional upsurge

Inspiration is accompanied by joy, a feeling of freedom and harmony.

Sources of inspiration#

Nature

Walking, watching the sunset, the sound of the sea or the rustling of leaves awaken a sense of connection with the world and fill you with ideas.

Art

A painting, a book, music or theatre can open new horizons for perception.

People

The stories, experiences and achievements of others inspire us to achieve our own.

Personal experience

Reflections, memories, emotions or inner discoveries can be a powerful source of inspiration.

Meditation and mindfulness

Silence and concentration help you hear your inner voice and open access to your deepest resources.

The role of inspiration in life#

Developing creativity

Inspiration triggers the process of creating something new – be it an idea, a project or a work of art.

Motivation and energy

It helps to overcome apathy and fears, filling you with confidence and strength to act.

Deepening connection with the world

Inspiration makes us feel part of something bigger, whether it's nature, a community, or the universe.

Enrichment of emotions

It enhances feelings of joy, gratitude and delight, making life rich and vibrant.

Inspiration in the context of TREE#

Collective inspiration

Joint practices, creativity, projects, and the exchange of ideas within the DREVO community become a powerful source of inspiration for participants.

Connection with nature

The natural rhythms and spaces created in DREVO promote inspiration through beauty and harmony.

Spiritual inspiration

Meditation, breathing exercises, and mindful practices deepen your connection with yourself and your highest values.

Art as a vehicle for inspiration

Through music, theatre, improvisation and visual art, DREVO participants find new sources of energy and ideas.

Inspiration through interaction

Interacting with other participants helps to open up new perspectives and enrich one's own perception.

How to find inspiration?#

Notice the details

Learn to see beauty in simple things: the play of light, the movement of the wind, the smile of a passerby.

Try something new

Experiment with different activities: travel, learn new skills, read unusual books.

Slow down

Allow yourself to stop to hear yourself, your breathing, your thoughts.

Surround yourself with inspiring people

Visit exhibitions, listen to music, communicate with people who awaken interest and enthusiasm in you.

Practice gratitude

Notice what you're grateful for in your life. This helps you tune into a positive flow of energy.

How to stay inspired?#

Write down ideas

Don't rely on memory - record your thoughts, even the most insignificant ones.

Act now

Use inspiration as a moment for immediate creativity or action so it doesn't fade away.

Maintain a routine

Regularly engaging in creative or hobbies creates a space where inspiration can emerge more frequently.

Share your inspiration

Share your ideas with others, it strengthens your sense of joy and motivates you to further develop.

Inspiration as a flow#

Listen to yourself

Tune into your inner voice that tells you what exactly inspires you.

Connect with the world

Inspiration is a flow of energy that comes through conscious interaction with the surrounding world.

Create

Don't be afraid to create. Even small steps pave the way to great inspiration.

Inspiration— it is a gift that fills us with the power to create, change and share light.

What inspires you? How do you use inspiration in your life?
