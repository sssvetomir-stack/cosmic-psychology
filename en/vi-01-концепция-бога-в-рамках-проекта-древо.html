The concept of God within the framework of the TREE project

The concept of God within the TREE project can be seen as an integration of a spiritual worldview with values ​​that unite culture, technology, health, cohabitation, and production.

God in the TREE— it's not a dogmatic concept, but rather a profound awareness of harmony and unity. Here are some possible key ideas:

God as the source of life and energy:
In terms of energy and health, God can be perceived as an eternal source that nourishes the body, mind, and soul. This is connected to the practices of conscious breathing, movement, and attention to the flow of life.

God as a collective consciousness:
In the TREE community, God can be represented through the idea of ​​collective intelligence, common purpose, and cooperation. This reflects the idea that we are all parts of a single whole, and by joining forces, we manifest the divine nature in our actions.

God's Values ​​through Culture:
Here, God can manifest through creativity, art, and self-expression. For example, in theater circles, jam tea, and improvisation, people connect with higher inspiration and reveal their inner, bright nature.

God as an ecological balance:
In the production and cohabitation group, God is perceived through nature and ecological harmony. Caring for the land, supporting farmers, and conscious consumption are manifestations of the divine through respect for the environment.

God as Inner Light and Personal Transformation:
Practices such as men's and women's circles help people discover their true nature, find balance and inner peace, which can also be considered part of the divine plan.

God as the source of creativity and creation

The creation of new forms of art, innovative technologies, or even ways of interacting with people is a manifestation of the divine, inspiring us to transcend the ordinary. At TREE, creativity can be not only a personal expression but also a collective process, where participants, by combining their ideas and efforts, create something greater. It can be a space where everyone feels like a co-creator of the world.

God as a Path to Awareness and Balance

The practices of meditation, conscious breathing, and physical exercise help a person connect with themselves and the world around them. Through this connection, a person can understand their place in the world and their unique role in the overall flow of life. In this sense, God is a state of inner balance, when the soul, mind, and body function in harmony.

God as values ​​embodied in actions

TREE offers not just theoretical reflection on God, but the active embodiment of divine values ​​in everyday life. This can be expressed through:

Ethics of cooperation: respect for everyone's work, honesty and support.

Social justice: providing equal opportunities for all participants, creating accessible solutions for everyone.

Eco-centric approach: careful use of resources, development of sustainable production.

God as an inspiration for the evolution of humanity

In the TREE, we can see God as an impetus for development—individual, collective, and universal. This is the idea that we not only live, but also develop, helping each other and creating a new generation that recognizes itself as part of a greater universe.

God as a mystery revealed through search

Finally, God in the TREE can be presented as a mystery that does not require a definitive answer, but rather encourages constant search and discovery. It can be a space for questions, dialogue, and reflection, where everyone can come to their own personal understanding of the highest.

God in the TREE concept can be both the personal experience of each participant and the unifying principle for all directions.

God in the concept of TREE– is a multifaceted idea that bridges traditional and modern notions of the supreme. In this concept, God is not only a transcendent force but also deeply integrated into everyday life, creativity, nature, and human interaction.

The main directions of the concept of God in the TREE:#

God as the source of life and energy
God manifests through the vital energy we experience through breathing, movement, and self-knowledge. Practices such as training, breathing exercises, and energy practices help TREE participants connect with this source, discover inner harmony, and strengthen their connection with the world.

God as a collective consciousness
In the TREE community, God is perceived as a unifying intelligence that manifests through cooperation and shared purpose. This idea holds that in a collective, each person is a part of a greater whole, and their combined efforts are capable of creating true miracles.

God through culture and creativity
Art, self-expression, and creativity are manifestations of divine inspiration. DREVO hosts theatrical improvisations, musical jams, and art workshops, where everyone can tap into their inner light and express it through their creativity.

God as an ecological balance
God is nature, to be admired, protected, and cooperating with. Supporting farmers, sustainable production, and responsible consumption are all seen as respect for the divine order of nature.

God as Personal Transformation
Men's and women's circles and events focused on spiritual growth help participants find inner balance and transform themselves. This process is a path to discovering the divine within through love, awareness, and balance.

God as inspiration for the future
TREE views God as a force that inspires the creation and development of new generations—those who will live in harmony with themselves, others, and nature. Projects focused on raising children, innovation, and co-creation are seen as the path to this new future.

The essence of the concept of God in the TREE:#

In this system, God is not so much an object of worship as an experience. It is the energy that unites people, nature, ideas, and practices into a single living tree of life. Each participant can find their own connection with God—through creativity, work, meditation, or caring for others.
