XII. Health and Sufficiency

Health and sufficiency— two key aspects of a harmonious life that are closely linked to physical, emotional, and spiritual well-being. As part of the project DREVOHealth is seen not only as the absence of disease, but also as a state of inner harmony, and sufficiency as a conscious approach to resources that ensures a balance between needs and reasonable consumption.

1. Health#

What is health?
Health is a state of complete physical, emotional and spiritual well-being that enables a person to live consciously and actively.

Health aspects:

Physical health:

Maintaining your body through proper nutrition, physical activity and prevention.

Emotional health:

Managing emotions, maintaining a positive attitude.

Spiritual health:

Awareness of one's connection with nature, people and higher values.

Practices for improving health:

Meditation and conscious breathing.

Movement and gymnastics.

Consumption of natural products.

Recovery through rest and sleep.

2. Sufficiency#

What is sufficiency?
Sufficiency is the awareness that a person needs exactly as much as is necessary for life, without excessive consumption and the desire for excess.

Sufficiency principles:

Conscious consumption:

Buying only the necessary things.

Respect for resources:

Preserving natural resources for future generations.

Balance between necessary and desirable:

Refusal of excesses in favor of inner satisfaction.

The path from the necessary to the sufficient:

Awareness of your true needs.

The ability to enjoy simple things.

Developing gratitude for what you already have.

3. The relationship between health and sufficiency#

How does sufficiency affect health?

Mindful consumption improves quality of life and reduces stress.

Minimizing excess consumption reduces the negative impact on the environment, which improves the health of society.

How does health help achieve sufficiency?

Physical and emotional harmony help you become more aware of your needs and avoid impulsive decisions.

4. Health and sufficiency practices in the DREVO project#

Healthy eating:

Organizing affordable and healthy meals made from natural ingredients.

Environmental initiatives:

Training in conscious use of resources and waste reduction.

Energy practices:

Participation in breathing and movement practices to restore internal balance.

Circles of Trust:

Discussion of topics related to sufficiency and conscious living.

Educational programs:

Seminars and trainings on health, nutrition and sustainable consumption.

5. The role of health and sufficiency for a harmonious society#

Physical and emotional well-being:

Harmonious people who care about their health contribute to improving the quality of life in the community.

Sustainable development:

Sufficiency reduces the pressure on natural resources, creating conditions for a sustainable future.

Emotional stability:

Being aware of your needs strengthens your sense of inner satisfaction and confidence.

Example for others:

The project's participants inspire others with their approach to health and conscious consumption.

6. How to start the path to health and sufficiency?#

Assess your needs:

Ask yourself: "What do I really need to be happy and well-off?"

Implement healthy habits:

Regular walks, proper nutrition, and a conscious approach to rest.

Reduce excess:

Review your purchases and habits, minimizing unnecessary things.

Learn gratitude:

Notice and appreciate what is already in your life.

Join the initiatives:

Participate in groups and projects focused on health and sustainability.

Conclusion#

Health and sufficiency— these are the foundations of a harmonious life that help a person find a balance between internal and external needs. In the project DREVOThese aspects are supported through practices, education and collective initiatives, inspiring participants to live a conscious and sustainable lifestyle.

What steps can you take to improve your health and move closer to a sense of sufficiency? How might these changes impact your life and the world around you?
