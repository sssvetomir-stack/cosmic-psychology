Cosmic Psychology

DREVO Cosmic Psychology is a worldview system that combines scientific knowledge, philosophy, cultural traditions, spiritual experience, and the study of the laws of human life. It is called Faith because it does not claim absolute scientific accuracy, but rather helps people understand their place in family, society, nature, and the Universe, find inner guidance, and direct their knowledge, abilities, and actions toward creation and development.

About the field

Everything is interconnected

Cosmic psychologyPsychology is an interdisciplinary field that studies humans as part of a unified system of nature, society, and the universe. It examines the psyche not in isolation, but in its relationship with biological, social, cultural, environmental, and astronomical factors.

The goal of cosmic psychology is to maintain and develop mental health, unlock personal potential, strengthen families and communities, and foster harmonious relationships with the world around us.

Main research areas:

heredity, pedigree and the influence of generations;

development of personality, character and values;

mental, emotional and physical health;

the influence of family, culture, traditions and collective consciousness;

the impact of natural cycles and astronomical phenomena on humans;

human interaction with the environment and ecosystems;

study of the phenomena of aura, biofields and other models of interaction proposed by various philosophical and spiritual traditions;

exploration of creativity, intuition, consciousness and spiritual development.

At the same time, scientifically proven knowledge, medical diagnostics and psychology are considered the basis of practical work, and philosophical and spiritual models are considered the subject of research and ideological understanding, and not as an established scientific fact.

Cosmic Psychology is a natural continuation and development of the New Psychology of DREVO. It is fully based on its fundamental principles, preserving the concept of the human being as a single living system in which the body, psyche, consciousness, heredity, social environment, nature, and spiritual development are interconnected.

At the same time, cosmic psychology expands the boundaries of research. It views humans not only as part of family, society, and the biosphere, but also as part of broader natural and cosmic processes. Its focus includes the influence of astronomical cycles, geophysical factors, collective consciousness, cultural traditions, the information environment, as well as issues of human interaction with the outside world at various organizational levels.

The new psychology of DREVO is the foundation of cosmic psychology, and cosmic psychology is its extension, combining knowledge from psychology, biology, ecology, anthropology, neuroscience, astronomy, philosophy, and other disciplines to form a holistic understanding of humanity and its place in the Universe.
