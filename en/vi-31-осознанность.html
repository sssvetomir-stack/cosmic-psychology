Mindfulness

MindfulnessMindfulness is a state of presence in the present moment, when a person is aware of their thoughts, feelings, actions, and the surrounding reality. It is the ability to observe oneself and the world without judgment, to be attentive to what is happening, and to make informed decisions. Mindfulness helps deepen self-understanding, improve the quality of life, and find harmony with the world around us.

What is mindfulness?#

Presence in the present

Be here and now, without being distracted by the past or worries about the future.

Observation without evaluation

Notice what is happening without judgment or emotional attachment.

Self-knowledge

Become aware of your thoughts, emotions and reactions to better understand yourself.

Attention management

The ability to focus on what is really important.

Connection with reality

Be in touch with the world around you, noticing its beauty and possibilities.

Why is mindfulness important?#

Improves emotional state

Mindfulness helps you cope with stress, anxiety and negative emotions.

Deepens self-knowledge

By becoming aware of one's thoughts and feelings, a person better understands one's desires and goals.

Improves quality of life

Being in the present moment allows you to enjoy life and notice its positive aspects.

Strengthens relationships

Mindfulness in communication makes interactions with people more sincere and profound.

Promotes spiritual growth

Mindfulness practice deepens your connection with yourself, nature, and higher values.

How to develop mindfulness?#

Meditation

Sit in a comfortable position, focus on your breathing, notice your thoughts without clinging to them.

Conscious breathing

Pay attention to each inhalation and exhalation, bringing yourself back to the here and now.

Slowing down the pace

Take pauses in your daily life to notice the details around you.

Mindfulness Diary

Write down your thoughts, feelings, and events of the day to better understand yourself.

Focus on feelings

While eating, walking or communicating, notice your feelings and reactions.

Gratitude practice

Every day, find moments you are grateful for and record them.

Body and movement

Do yoga, dance, or other body practices, noticing how you feel your body.

Examples of mindfulness in everyday life#

During communication

Listen to your interlocutor carefully, without being distracted by your own thoughts or plans.

At work

Complete tasks with focus, avoiding multitasking.

During meals

Eat slowly, noticing the taste, texture and aroma of your food.

In nature

Notice the colors, sounds, smells and textures around you.

In times of stress

Take a pause, focus on your breathing and assess the situation from the perspective of an observer.

Mindfulness in the context of TREE#

Spiritual practices

DREVO participants use meditation, breathing exercises, and movement to deepen awareness.

Conscious interaction

Relationships in the community are built on attentiveness, respect and sincerity.

Natural connection

Mindfulness in nature helps participants deepen their connection with the world around them.

Creative expression

Art created in a state of awareness becomes a way of expressing the inner world.

Conscious consumption

Use resources wisely and with respect for nature.

Benefits of Mindfulness#

Emotional stability

Mindfulness helps you cope with negative emotions and find inner balance.

Improving physical health

Mindfulness practice reduces stress levels and strengthens the immune system.

Harmony in relationships

Mindful communication builds trust and understanding.

Conscious decisions

A person makes more thoughtful and wiser decisions.

Spiritual development

Mindfulness deepens your connection with yourself and the Universe, opening new horizons of understanding.

Mindfulness as a spiritual principle#

Presence in the moment

Mindfulness teaches us to be “here and now,” which opens access to inner peace.

Acceptance of reality

By noticing things as they are, a person gets rid of fear, illusions and resistance.

Harmony with the world

Mindfulness deepens the understanding of the interconnectedness of all things.

The path to truth

The practice of mindfulness helps us see things as they are, without distortion.

Mindfulness— is the ability to be in harmony with oneself, to notice the beauty of life and to accept the world as it is.

How do you practice mindfulness? What moments in your life help you stay "here and now"?
