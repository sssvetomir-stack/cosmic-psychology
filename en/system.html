Space Psychology DREVO#

Introduction#

Space Psychology DREVO— is an extension of the New Psychology DREVO, which views a person not as an isolated individual, but as part of a family, clan, society, nature, the Earth and the Universe.

It combines the philosophy of man, the psychology of personality, ancestral memory, an ecological worldview and an understanding of man’s place in the universe.

Cosmic psychology is based on the main law of DREVO:

Everything is interconnected.

A person influences a family.
Family influences the Kin.
The clan shapes culture and society.
Society changes nature and the environment.
The surrounding world, in turn, again influences a person.

Therefore, it is impossible to fully understand a person separately from the system of connections in which he lives.

1. The place of Cosmic Psychology in the DREVO system#

The DREVO worldview system consists of three interconnected foundations.

Cosmic Faith DREVO#

It is a worldview and philosophy that defines:

  • man's attitude to the Universe;
  • understanding the unity of life;
  • universal values;
  • the meaning of development;
  • responsibility to nature and future generations.
  • Cosmic Faith answers the question:
  • What is man's place in the Universe and what does he live for?

New Psychology DREVO#

This is the science and philosophy of man.

She studies:

  • body;
  • subconscious;
  • psyche;
  • consciousness;
  • will;
  • soul;
  • spirit;
  • values;
  • relationship;
  • social and natural environment.
  • New Psychology DREVO answers the question:
  • How is a person structured and by what laws does his personality develop?

Ancestral Power#

This is the tradition, culture, rituals and social structure of the Clans.

It includes:

  • ancestral memory;
  • continuity of generations;
  • brotherhood;
  • mutual support;
  • transfer of knowledge;
  • joint work;
  • creation of cooperatives and associations;
  • unions between representatives of Clans;
  • maintaining traditions and common obligations.

In the text “The Faith of Ancestors “Family Power””, the family structure is associated with life according to conscience, love, harmony with nature, the creation of brotherhoods, Family Circles, cooperative unions, educational projects and a system of mutual responsibility.

"Ancestral Power" answers the question:

How are worldviews and knowledge about a person embodied in the family, clan and community?

Cosmic psychology#

Cosmic psychology unites these three directions.

She is considering:

  • Cosmic Faith as a source of meaning and values;
  • New psychology DREVO as a model of man;
  • Ancestral Power as a historical, cultural and social environment for the development of personality.

2. Definition of Cosmic Psychology#

Space Psychology DREVO— is a philosophical and psychological system that studies human development in relation to its body, inner world, family, society, nature, planetary environment, and concept of the Universe.

Its task is to help a person:

  • understand your inner nature;
  • to understand one’s place in the family, clan and society;
  • to form mature values;
  • develop will and responsibility;
  • find the meaning of life;
  • learn to cooperate;
  • move from consumption to creation;
  • leave a useful legacy for future generations.

Cosmic psychology views personal development not only as achieving personal success, but also as increasing a person's ability to support life around them.

4. Multidimensional structure of a person#

Body#

The body is the material basis of life.

Through the body a person:

  • perceives the world;
  • is valid;
  • experiences sensations;
  • accumulates physical experience;
  • interacts with the natural environment.
  • Psychological development should not come at the expense of physical health.

Subconscious#

The subconscious stores automatic reactions, fears, habits, emotional patterns and a significant portion of past experience.

It may manifest itself as:

  • early life experiences;
  • family patterns of behavior;
  • cultural attitudes;
  • learned methods of protection;
  • unconscious needs;
  • internal contradictions.
  • Development does not require suppression of the subconscious, but its gradual awareness and transformation.

Psyche#

The psyche connects sensations, emotions, memory, attention, imagination and thinking.

It allows a person to adapt to the external environment and at the same time create their own internal picture of the world.

Consciousness#

Consciousness gives you the opportunity to observe yourself, analyze what is happening, compare options and make a choice.

Consciousness expands the space of inner freedom.

Will#

Will transforms a conscious decision into action.

Without will, knowledge remains intention. With will, values ​​become actions, and decisions become a sustainable life direction.

Soul#

In the system of Cosmic Psychology, the concept of soul refers to the deep individuality of a person, his or her ability to love, empathize, seek meaning, and feel an inner connection with life.

This is a philosophical and spiritual level of the model, not an established scientific category.

Spirit#

The spirit expresses a person’s orientation towards truth, freedom, dignity, overcoming limitations and serving higher values.

It manifests itself in the ability to maintain an inner core, act according to one’s conscience, and continue to create even in difficult circumstances.

Values#

Values ​​determine why a person uses his knowledge, abilities and freedom.

A developed intellect without moral compass can become a destructive force. Therefore, maturity is determined not only by a person's capabilities but also by the direction in which they are applied.

Relationship#

Personality is formed through relationships with parents, children, partners, mentors, friends, community and society.

Through relationships a person learns:

  • trust;
  • respect;
  • responsibility;
  • cooperation;
  • border protection;
  • care;
  • mutual assistance.

Wednesday#

A person is constantly exposed to:

  • nature;
  • family;
  • culture;
  • education;
  • information space;
  • economy;
  • technologies;
  • social norms.
  • Therefore, personal development cannot be separated from the psychological and environmental environment.

5. Laws of personality development in Cosmic Psychology#

1. The Law of Integrity#

A human being is a single living system.

The body, psyche, consciousness, will, values, relationships and environment mutually influence each other.

True development strengthens the entire system, not one part of it at the expense of the destruction of the rest.

2. The law of cause and effect#

Thoughts influence emotions.
Emotions influence decisions.
Decisions become actions.
Actions form habits.
Habits strengthen character.
Character influences life path.

Awareness of internal causes allows a person to gradually change the consequences of their actions.

3. The Law of Conscious Choice#

Freedom develops with awareness.

Consciousness helps to see possible paths.
Values ​​help determine direction.
Willpower helps to implement a decision.

The more a person understands his motives, the less he is controlled by random impulses and external pressure.

4. Law of Responsibility#

Freedom is impossible without responsibility.

A mature person is aware of their involvement in what is happening, is responsible for their words and actions, admits mistakes and tries to correct the harm caused.

Responsibility gives a person back the ability to influence his own life.

5. The law of continuous development#

A person changes throughout his life.

Not only actions, but also inactions shape personality. Every day strengthens some qualities and weakens others.

6. The Law of Small Steps#

Sustainable development is created by small but regular actions.

Repetition turns an action into a habit, a habit into a quality, and a quality into a part of one's character.

7. The Law of Overcoming#

Difficulties do not automatically make a person stronger.

Growth occurs when a person makes sense of a challenge, draws conclusions, changes strategy, seeks support, and turns the experience into maturity.

8. The Law of Meaning#

A person is capable of enduring significant difficulties when he understands why he is acting.

Meaning unites efforts, values ​​and life direction.

9. Law of Cooperation#

Personality develops through interaction with other people.

Collaboration, honest feedback, knowledge sharing, trust, and mutual support empower each participant.

10. The Law of Psychological Ecology#

Everything that a person regularly lets into his life influences his inner world.

Information, music, communication, culture, the digital environment and nature are gradually becoming part of mental life.

Therefore, a person needs to consciously shape his environment.

11. The Law of Creation#

Development reaches maturity when a person's abilities begin to benefit others.

Creativity can manifest itself in family, raising children, work, science, art, entrepreneurship, restoration of nature and the transfer of knowledge.

12. Law of Inheritance#

The highest form of maturity is the ability to create conditions for the development of future generations.

Heritage is not only material things, but also:

  • knowledge;
  • culture;
  • healthy relationships;
  • restored nature;
  • created organizations;
  • traditions;
  • good works;
  • transmitted experience.

6. The Main Law of Cosmic Psychology#

By developing oneself, one changes the world. By changing the world, one again changes oneself.

Personal development cannot be considered complete if it leads to the destruction of family, society, nature or internal health.

Genuine development strengthens the links between:

  • body and mind;
  • freedom and responsibility;
  • individual and community;
  • generations;
  • man and nature;
  • knowledge and morality;
  • technology and life.

7. Generic psychology#

Ancestral psychology is an important area of ​​DREVO Cosmic Psychology.

She studies how a person is influenced by:

  • family history;
  • intergenerational relations;
  • family traditions;
  • transmitted behavior patterns;
  • ancestral memory;
  • cultural affiliation;
  • ideas about duty and responsibility;
  • experience of ancestors.

"Ancestral Power" suggests not only remembering ancestors, but also consciously representing one's Family, creating alliances between families, maintaining agreements, developing brotherhoods, joint work, and the transfer of experience.

At the same time, clan affiliation should not turn into the superiority of one clan over another.

A mature understanding of the Family implies:

  • respect for origin;
  • preservation of useful heritage;
  • understanding of generic errors;
  • liberation from destructive patterns;
  • responsibility to descendants;
  • cooperation with other families and Clans.

Ancestral Power lies not only in the past. It manifests itself in the ability to create a worthy continuation of the family line in the present and future.

8. Cosmic consciousness#

Cosmic consciousnessIn the DREVO system, this is a person’s expanded understanding of their place in the world.

It begins with self-awareness, but does not end with personal interests.

The levels of expansion of consciousness can be represented as follows:

  • Awareness of the body and needs.
  • Awareness of emotions and internal reactions.
  • Awareness of thoughts and decisions.
  • Awareness of values ​​and responsibility.
  • Awareness of family and clan.
  • Awareness of community and society.
  • Awareness of connection with nature and the Earth.
  • Awareness of belonging to humanity.
  • Awareness of man as a part of the Universe.
  • Awareness of responsibility for the future.

Cosmic consciousness does not mean abandoning everyday life. On the contrary, it should manifest itself in concrete decisions, work, relationships, and care for the world around us.

  • the connection of man with the Universe;
  • the value of knowledge;

10. Attitude to nature#

In Cosmic Psychology, nature is not only the environment, but also a condition for the formation of a person.

Natural cycles remind us of:

  • birth and completion;
  • activity and rest;
  • growth and restoration;
  • interdependence of living systems;
  • limited resources;
  • the need for balance.
  • Humans are not absolute masters of nature. They are part of it and are responsible for maintaining living conditions.
  • Caring for nature is also caring for:
  • physical health;
  • psychological stability;
  • quality of society;
  • future generations;
  • own inner world.

11. Technology and human development#

Cosmic psychology does not oppose technology to nature.

She asks the question:

  • Does this technology enhance or destroy life?
  • Technologies must:
  • to free people from hard and dangerous work;
  • maintain health;
  • expand access to knowledge;
  • help restore nature;
  • strengthen cooperation;
  • increase the independence of communities;
  • preserve human dignity.

Technological advancement without responsibility creates danger. Therefore, technological progress must be accompanied by moral, psychological, and social development.

12. Practices of Cosmic Psychology#

Cosmic psychology can be embodied through simple everyday practices.

Self-observation#

Observing thoughts, emotions, habits and physical states without hasty judgment.

Conscious choice#

Regularly check your decisions with questions:

  • Why am I doing this?
  • What are the possible consequences?
  • Does this align with my values?
  • Does this strengthen life?

Psychological ecology#

Conscious choice of information, communication, culture and the digital environment.

Connection with nature#

Observing natural cycles, working with the land, walking, restoring natural spaces and using resources responsibly.

Ancestral memory#

Studying family history, preserving memories, documents, knowledge, crafts and family traditions.

Cooperation#

Creation of support circles, educational groups, cooperatives, creative and community projects.

Creative work#

Developing one's own abilities through activities that are beneficial to people, communities and nature.

Transfer of experience#

Educating children, supporting young people, mentoring and preserving knowledge for future generations.

Contemplation of the Universe#

Observing the starry sky, studying astronomy, and reflecting on the scale of the universe as a practice for expanding one's worldview and overcoming excessive self-focus.

13. Scientific and philosophical levels#

For the integrity of Cosmic Psychology it is necessary to distinguish several levels of statements.

Scientifically researched level#

These include:

  • personality psychology;
  • physiology;
  • neurobiology;
  • influence of social environment;
  • family patterns of behavior;
  • habit formation;
  • impact of stress;
  • influence of nature and information environment;
  • mechanisms for learning and collaboration.

Philosophical level#

These include:

  • the meaning of life;
  • human integrity;
  • the place of the individual in the Universe;
  • responsibility to future generations;
  • the idea of ​​harmony;
  • moral purpose of development.

Spiritual and ideological level#

These include:

  • soul;
  • spirit;
  • Cosmic Faith;
  • experience of unity with nature and the Universe;
  • symbolic meaning of rituals;
  • personal experience of connection with ancestors.
  • These levels may interact, but should not be passed off as one another.

A philosophical or spiritual belief doesn't become a scientific fact simply because it's important to a person. At the same time, the lack of scientific confirmation doesn't preclude it from being considered a personal experience, a cultural symbol, or a philosophical hypothesis.

14. The Purpose of Cosmic Psychology#

The goal of DREVO Cosmic Psychology is to develop a holistic, free, responsible and creative personality.

Such a personality:

  • understands himself;
  • takes care of body and mental health;
  • knows how to manage attention and behavior;
  • is aware of his values;
  • accepts responsibility;
  • respects family and clan;
  • able to cooperate;
  • maintains connection with nature;
  • uses technology for good;
  • seeks knowledge;
  • creates a useful legacy;
  • thinks not only about today, but also about future generations.

15. Formula of Cosmic Psychology DREVO#

To know yourself means to see the inner world of a person.
To understand your Family means to see the movement of life through generations.
To understand society is to see the power of human connections.
To understand nature is to see the system of which we are a part.
To understand the Universe means to realize the scale of life and the responsibility of man for his path.

17

Conclusion#

DREVO's cosmic psychology combines a person's inner development with their responsibility to family, Kin, society, nature, and the future.

She doesn't call for people to abandon earthly life for the sake of abstract cosmos. On the contrary, she asserts that a connection with the Universe manifests itself in the most concrete actions:

  • in relation to one's own body;
  • in honesty;
  • in caring for loved ones;
  • in fulfilling obligations;
  • in joint work;
  • in nature conservation;
  • in the creation of knowledge and culture;
  • in responsibility for what remains after us.

Cosmic psychology is a path from self-awareness to awareness of the whole, and from awareness of the whole to a more mature, responsible, and creative life.
