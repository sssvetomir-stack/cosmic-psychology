Harmony with nature

Harmony with natureHarmony with nature is a conscious and respectful interaction with the surrounding world, based on an understanding of the interconnectedness of humans and nature. It is the ability to live in harmony with natural cycles, preserving its resources and maintaining ecological balance. Harmony with nature gives a sense of unity, inner peace, and responsibility for the future of the planet.

What is harmony with nature?#

Unity with the world

The realization that man is a part of nature, not its master, and his actions directly affect the planet.

Respect for natural rhythms

Living in accordance with the cycles of nature: the change of seasons, day and night, natural rhythms.

Maintaining balance

Using the earth's resources with care so as not to disturb the ecological balance.

Caring for the future

Understanding that actions today affect the state of nature for future generations.

A feeling of gratitude

Respect and gratitude for what nature provides: food, water, air, materials and inspiration.

Why is it important to be in harmony with nature?#

Physical health

Nature provides us with fresh air, clean water and natural products that improve our health.

Psychological balance

Being in nature calms you, relieves stress, and improves your mood.

Preservation of the ecosystem

Respect for nature prevents the destruction of biological systems on which human life depends.

Spiritual development

Harmony with nature helps us feel connected to the world, deepening our spiritual perception of life.

The future of the planet

Caring for nature ensures its preservation for future generations.

Harmony with nature in the context of TREE#

Environmental awareness

DREVO members strive to minimize negative impacts on nature through responsible consumption and eco-friendly practices.

Natural practices

Inspiration from nature is used in meditation, movement, creativity and learning.

Creation of eco-spaces

Combining life and nature in one space: creating gardens, eco-friendly buildings, and using renewable energy sources.

Seasonal rhythms

Life in TREE is organized according to natural cycles, including nutrition, practices, and events.

Education and upbringing

Through training, participants impart knowledge about nature, ecology, and harmonious interaction with the surrounding world.

How to achieve harmony with nature?#

Awareness of the connection

Recognize that you are a part of nature and your actions affect its state.

Eco-friendly behavior

Minimize plastic use, sort waste, save resources.

Staying in nature

Take regular walks in the forest, near the water, observe nature, meditate in the fresh air.

Seasonal nutrition

Eat more seasonal and local foods to maintain natural balance.

Creativity and inspiration

Use nature as a source of ideas for art, crafts, and other creative activities.

Caring for the land

Plant a tree, tend a garden, participate in eco-campaigns, or create your own initiatives.

Respect animals

Respect for animals is an important part of harmony with nature.

Examples of harmony with nature#

Eco-life

Use of solar energy, eco-materials and renewable resources.

Joint farming

Growing food without chemicals and caring for the fertility of the soil.

Conscious tourism

Travel with minimal environmental impact.

Traditional practices

A return to ancient farming methods, herbal collection and natural healing.

Natural rituals

Conducting rituals and ceremonies associated with the veneration of earth, water and fire.

Benefits of Harmony with Nature#

Energy and inspiration

Nature fills a person with strength, improves his mood and gives a creative impulse.

Strengthening health

Natural products, clean air and an active lifestyle contribute to longevity.

Inner peace

Nature helps restore balance, relieve stress and deepen spiritual awareness.

Keeping the peace

Harmony with nature teaches respect for life in all its manifestations.

Harmony with nature as part of the spiritual path#

Connection to the ground

Feel your connection to the earth through meditation, barefoot walking, and mindful eating.

Awareness of cycles

Respect the rhythms of nature, adapt your life to the changing seasons and times of day.

Spiritual cleansing

Water, fire, air and earth can be used in cleansing and restoration practices.

Recognition of interdependence

Understanding that everything in the world is connected helps us live more consciously and respectfully.

Harmony with nature— is a path to a deep connection with yourself and the world, which helps you find inner peace, improve your health, and understand your place in the great cycle of life.
