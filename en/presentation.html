Cosmic Psychology

Cosmic Faith DREVO— is a worldview and philosophy. It defines a person's attitude toward life, nature, humanity, and the universe, and also shapes universal values ​​that unite people regardless of their origin, culture, and religious beliefs.

Scroll ↓
01
Key idea

Worldview

The Cosmic Faith views humanity as part of a single, living Universe. Humans do not exist separately from nature, society, and the cosmos: they are integrated into a unified system of interconnections and are responsible for the consequences of their actions.

This worldview helps a person to realize:

your place in the world;

connection with nature and other people;

responsibility to future generations;

the need for internal and social development;

the value of life in all its manifestations.

02
Key idea

Philosophy

The philosophy of Cosmic Faith is based on the search for meaning, truth, harmony and understanding of the laws of the universe.

It connects:

human spiritual experience;

philosophical understanding of life;

nature observation;

cultural wisdom of peoples;

scientific knowledge;

striving for continuous development.

Cosmic Faith doesn't offer ready-made answers to all questions. It encourages people to explore the world, reflect, develop their consciousness, and test their beliefs through personal experience, knowledge, and creative activity.

03
Key idea

Attitude to the Universe

The Universe is perceived as a great whole, of which the Earth, nature, humanity and each individual person are a part.

The attitude towards the Universe is expressed through:

respect for its laws and cycles;

study of space and nature;

awareness of the limitations of human knowledge;

openness to new discoveries;

a feeling of gratitude for life;

the desire to live in harmony with the surrounding world.

Man is seen asmicrocosm— a living system that reflects the natural, social and spiritual processes of the greater world.

04
Key idea

Universal values

DREVO's Cosmic Faith is based on values ​​that can be understood and shared by people of different cultures:

Life— respect and protection of life in all its manifestations.

Love- the ability to care, support and create.

Conscience- an internal sense of responsibility for one's own decisions and actions.

Truth— the desire for honest knowledge of oneself and the world.

Freedom— the right of a person to choose his own path without violating the freedom of others.

Responsibility- understanding the consequences of one's actions for family, society, nature and future generations.

Justice- respect for human dignity and the desire for honest relationships.

Knowledge- continuous learning, research and sharing of accumulated experience.

Creation— work and creativity aimed at the development of life.

Harmony- achieving balance between body, soul, mind, society, nature and technology.

Unity in Diversity- respect for different peoples, cultures, traditions and paths of spiritual development.

05
Key idea

Brief formula

The DREVO Cosmic Faith is a worldview of unity, a philosophy of knowledge, a respectful attitude toward the Universe, and a system of universal values ​​that guides a person toward a conscious, responsible, and creative life.
