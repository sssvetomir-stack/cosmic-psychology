Pride

PridePride is a complex emotion that can have both positive and negative connotations. In its deepest, constructive form, pride is associated with self-respect, satisfaction with one's achievements, and recognition of one's own efforts. In its destructive form, it can express itself through arrogance, egotism, and disregard for others.

What is pride?#

Creative pride

Awareness of one's successes and achievements, a feeling of satisfaction from the efforts made.

It is an inner confidence based on real values ​​and work.

Destructive pride (arrogance)

A feeling of superiority over others, a tendency to exaggerate one's own merits and belittle the importance of other people.

Pride as self-respect

It helps a person maintain self-respect, act with dignity and not allow others to humiliate him.

Pride as a sense of belonging

It is the joy of belonging to something bigger: a family, a community, a culture or a nation.

The role of pride in human life#

Motivation

Creative pride motivates a person to develop, achieve goals and share the results with others.

Self-assessment

It strengthens your sense of self-worth and helps you realize your value.

Strength to overcome difficulties

Feeling proud of yourself or your loved ones can inspire you to overcome difficult situations.

Destruction of relationships (in case of pride)

Excessive pride (hubris) can lead to alienation, conflict and isolation.

Pride in the context of TREE#

Creative pride in community

DREVO members can feel proud of their shared achievements, joint projects, and each person's contribution to the common cause.

Pride as inspiration

Feeling proud of personal or community success motivates other participants to take action and develop.

Avoiding Pride

In a community, it is important to remember equality and respect so that pride does not degenerate into arrogance.

Pride in nature and its conservation

DREVO members can be proud of their care for nature, their environmental achievements, and their contribution to harmony with the world around them.

How to develop healthy pride?#

Acknowledge your efforts

Notice your achievements, even small ones, and feel satisfaction from what you have done.

Keep your balance

Remember that your successes are important, but so are the efforts of other people.

Be grateful

The feeling of pride will become more harmonious if you remember the help you received from others.

Share your achievements

Use your successes to inspire others, not to rise above them.

Study your emotions

Distinguish between creative pride and destructive pride to avoid arrogance and selfishness.

What is the difference between pride and arrogance?#

Pride

Based on real achievements.

Maintains respect for oneself and others.

Inspires and strengthens harmony.

Pride

More often than not, it is based on the desire to appear better than others.

Reduces empathy and causes conflict.

Leads away from true self-realization.

Pride as a part of inner growth#

Feeling proud helps us recognize our value and worth, but it is important to remain humble and respectful of others.

Being mindful of pride allows you to use it as a tool for personal growth, motivation, and inspiration.

Pride- This is a bright feeling when it is based on respect for oneself and others, and it becomes dangerous when it turns into pride.

How do you understand pride? Which of its manifestations inspire you, and which ones make you wary?
