Laws of personality development#

New Psychology of DREVO#

Introduction#

Personality development is not a random process. It follows certain patterns that manifest themselves regardless of a person's age, profession, social status, or cultural background.

In New Psychology DREVO these patterns are calledlaws of personality development.

They are not rigid prescriptions and do not promise a uniform path for everyone. They are general principles, the understanding of which helps a person become more self-aware, make mature decisions, and purposefully build their life.

1. Law of Integrity#

A human being is a single living system.

The body, subconscious, psyche, consciousness, will, soul, spirit, values, relationships and environment constantly interact and influence each other.

Changes in one part of the personality inevitably affect other levels. Therefore, the development of one quality should not come at the expense of health, relationships, moral compass, or inner balance.

True development does not divide a person into separate parts, but strengthens the entire system.

2. The law of cause and effect#

Every thought influences a person’s emotional state.

Emotions influence decisions.
Decisions become actions.
Repeated actions become habits.
Habits shape character.
Character largely determines one's life path.

Not every event depends on a person, but he is able to understand the reasons for his own reactions, decisions and actions.

By understanding the reasons for what is happening within oneself, a person gains the ability to influence the consequences.

3. The Law of Conscious Choice#

Freedom begins where conscious choice appears.

A person cannot always change circumstances, but he or she is able to develop their attitude towards them, evaluate possible solutions, and choose a course of action.

Consciousness helps to see various possibilities.
Values ​​help us determine which choices are right.
Willpower helps to implement the decision made.

The higher a person's awareness, the less dependent he is on random impulses, external pressure and mindless habits.

4. Law of Responsibility#

Every freedom comes with responsibility.

A person becomes mature when he stops constantly looking for someone to blame and begins to realize his own participation in what is happening.

Responsibility means being willing to take responsibility for your thoughts, words, decisions and actions.

It also implies the ability to:

  • admit mistakes;
  • to repair the damage caused;
  • fulfill the obligations assumed;
  • accept the consequences of your decisions;
  • develop positive results of one's own actions.
  • Responsibility does not humiliate a person, but rather gives him back the ability to influence his own life.

5. The law of continuous development#

Development continues throughout life.

Every day we live leaves a mark on our body, psyche, character, relationships and worldview.

A person either strengthens certain qualities or gradually loses them. Even inaction is a form of choice and, over time, changes personality.

Therefore, development cannot be reduced to isolated periods of learning or achievements. It occurs continually—through thoughts, habits, decisions, work, relationships, and life experiences.

6. The Law of Small Steps#

Big changes come from small daily actions.

A rare emotional impulse can give a person inspiration, but it is consistency that creates a lasting result.

A small action, repeated regularly, gradually becomes a habit. A habit strengthens a certain quality, and the quality becomes part of one's character.

Therefore, successive small steps often prove stronger than sudden but short-term efforts.

7. The Law of Overcoming#

Growth is impossible without facing challenges.

However, trials in themselves do not make a person stronger. Some difficulties can strengthen a person, while others can destroy them.

Development is determined by how a person responds to challenges:

  • what is he learning;
  • what conclusions does he draw;
  • does he seek help;
  • does it maintain dignity;
  • is able to change strategy;
  • does the experience gained transform into maturity?
  • Every difficulty consciously overcome increases a person’s experience, resilience and inner strength.

8. The Law of Meaning#

A person is capable of overcoming difficulties for a long time if he understands why he is doing it.

Meaning unites one's efforts, values, and direction in life. It helps one endure temporary constraints, remain faithful to one's chosen path, and see the significance of one's actions.

Without meaning, even an outwardly successful life can feel empty.

Meaning transforms effort into development, work into service, and individual events into part of a more holistic life journey.

9. Law of Cooperation#

Personality develops in interaction with other people.

Knowledge sharing, mutual support, teamwork, creativity, trust, and honest feedback enhance the development of each participant.

Other people help a person:

  • see your own strengths and weaknesses;
  • gain new experience;
  • learn responsibility;
  • develop listening skills;
  • overcome egocentrism;
  • create results that are inaccessible to one person.
  • True growth does not isolate a person from society, but makes him more mature, independent and useful to others.

10. The Law of Psychological Ecology#

Everything that a person regularly lets into his life gradually becomes part of his inner world.

The mental state is influenced by:

  • information;
  • communication;
  • music;
  • culture;
  • language;
  • nutrition;
  • nature;
  • work environment;
  • digital space;
  • the emotional atmosphere of family and community.

A person is not capable of completely isolating himself from the external environment, but he can consciously choose a significant part of it.

Therefore, caring for psychological ecology is just as important as caring for the body and physical health.

11. The Law of Creation#

Development reaches maturity when a person begins not only to receive, but also to create.

Creation can manifest itself in different ways:

  • in raising children;
  • scientific research;
  • art;
  • craft;
  • entrepreneurship;
  • restoration of nature;
  • helping people;
  • community organizations;
  • transfer of knowledge and experience.
  • Creation turns human abilities into a source of benefit.

A person is most fully revealed when their knowledge, work, talent and life experience begin to support the development of other people and the world around them.

12. Law of Inheritance#

The highest form of development is the ability to leave behind not only material results, but also the conditions for the continuation of life and development.

A person's legacy may include:

  • knowledge;
  • culture;
  • good works;
  • well-behaved children;
  • healthy relationships;
  • restored nature;
  • created organizations;
  • useful technologies;
  • transferred experience;
  • preserved memory of the Family.

A mature person thinks not only about his own well-being, but also about the consequences his life will have for future generations.

A true legacy continues to benefit even after a person's personal journey has ended.

The main law of DREVO#

Everything is interconnected#

By developing oneself, a person influences the family.

By developing the family, he influences the clan and the immediate community.

By developing the community, he influences society.

By developing society, it changes culture, economy, technology and attitude towards nature.

By changing the world around us, a person again changes himself.

Therefore, true personal development is not the pursuit of personal perfection at any cost. It is the continuous strengthening of harmony between one's inner world, one's body, relationships, society, nature, and future generations.

The new psychology of DREVO sees this relationship as the basis:

  • health;
  • freedom;
  • maturity;
  • responsibility;
  • creation;
  • sustainable future.
  • Personal development becomes complete when a person’s inner growth strengthens the life around him.

A comprehensive study of personality and human condition#

To understand a person's individual characteristics, the servants of the Cosmic Faith strive to create the most complete picture of their personality, life path, and state of health.

Such studies are conducted exclusively with the voluntary, informed, and conscious consent of the individual. The individual independently determines what information they are willing to provide and may withdraw from participation or limit the scope of information reviewed at any time.

A comprehensive study may include:

  • pedigree and family history;
  • features of upbringing and family relationships;
  • lifestyle;
  • nutrition, sleep and physical activity;
  • professional and social environment;
  • psychological and emotional state;
  • results of medical examinations;
  • extensive laboratory tests, including blood tests;
  • information about physical and mental health;
  • personal values, abilities, interests and life goals.

Medical information is processed only with legal grounds, voluntary consent, and confidentiality. Its professional interpretation must be performed by qualified medical professionals.

If the person so desires, traditional cultural and symbolic practices of self-knowledge can also be included in the overall picture, for example:

  • astrological birth chart;
  • observation of the features of the iris of the eye within the framework of the iridological tradition;
  • study of lines and shapes of palms;
  • In certain cultural practices, observing the characteristics of the feet.

These approaches are not considered independent medical diagnostic methods. They can be used solely as elements of a personal, cultural, or ideological tradition, as a source of reflection and discussion of individual life experiences.

The results of such practices should not:

  • replace a medical examination;
  • used to make a diagnosis;
  • serve as a basis for cancellation or prescription of treatment;
  • restrict human rights;
  • determine its value, destiny or social status;
  • be used without his voluntary consent.
  • DREVO's cosmic psychology distinguishes three levels of human study:

Scientific and medical level#

Includes methods based on verifiable data: medical examinations, laboratory diagnostics, psychological assessment and observation by qualified specialists.

Psychological and biographical level#

Includes the study of a person's life experiences, family history, lifestyle, relationships, habits, values, abilities, challenges, and goals.

Cultural and symbolic level#

Includes traditional self-knowledge practices voluntarily chosen by an individual that may have personal, spiritual, or cultural significance, but are not presented as scientifically proven diagnostics.

The main goal of such a comprehensive approach is not to impose a ready-made definition of their personality on a person, but to help them better understand themselves, to see the connections between health, lifestyle, family history, internal states and life decisions.

A person cannot be reduced to a diagnosis, analysis, family tree, or symbolic map. Each source of information is only a separate part of a broader and constantly evolving picture of the individual.
