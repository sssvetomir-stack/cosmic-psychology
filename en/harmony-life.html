Preamble

Project DREVO is a path to a harmonious life based on mindfulness, spirituality, and respect for nature. It is founded on principles that help individuals strengthen their connection with themselves, society, and the world around them. This concept unites spiritual and environmental values, inspiring personal development, the creation of a sustainable community, and a commitment to the future. The work reveals key ideas and practices that enable everyone to contribute to a harmonious and sustainable life.
