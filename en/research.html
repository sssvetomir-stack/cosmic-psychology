Cosmic psychology#

Cosmic psychology— is an interdisciplinary scientific and philosophical field that studies humans as part of a unified system of nature, society, and the Universe. It examines the individual not in isolation, but in relation to biological, psychological, social, cultural, environmental, and planetary processes.

The main goal of Cosmic Psychology is to develop a holistic understanding of humanity, combining the achievements of modern science with a philosophical understanding of humanity's place in the world.

The main goal of Cosmic Psychology is to maintain and strengthen mental and physical health, unlock personal potential, develop a responsible worldview, strengthen family and society, and foster harmonious relationships with nature and the surrounding world.

Main research areas#

Cosmic psychology studies:

  • heredity, pedigree and the influence of generations on personality development;
  • formation of character, values, worldview and life goals;
  • mental, emotional, physical and social well-being of a person;
  • the influence of family, culture, education, traditions and collective social processes;
  • human interaction with the natural environment and ecosystems;
  • the influence of natural, climatic, geophysical and astronomical factors on human life;
  • mechanisms of consciousness, thinking, creativity, intuition and personal development;
  • spiritual and philosophical ideas about man that have developed in various cultures and civilizations.

A separate area of ​​research is the analysis of concepts such as aura, biofield, vital energy, human field interaction, and other models found in the philosophical, religious, and esoteric traditions of various peoples. These concepts are considered part of the world's cultural heritage and a subject for philosophical analysis. Their study does not constitute acceptance of their scientific validity and does not replace the results of research based on modern science.

Methodological foundations#

Cosmic psychology is based on the principle of distinguishing between different levels of knowledge.

Scientific levelincludes psychology, medicine, neuroscience, biology, sociology, ecology, and other disciplines that use verifiable research methods.

Philosophical levelexamines questions of the meaning of life, freedom, responsibility, values, man's place in the Universe and the relationship of the individual with the surrounding world.

Spiritual and cultural levelstudies traditions, symbolic systems, religious experience and various ideas about the inner nature of man that have formed in world culture.

Each of these levels has its own research methods and evaluation criteria. Cosmic psychology does not conflate them, but rather views them as complementary ways of understanding the human being.

The basic principle#

Cosmic psychology is based on the principle of integrity:

A person is part of an interconnected system that includes their own body, family, kin, society, nature, the Earth, and the Universe. By changing themselves, a person influences the world around them, and changes in the world inevitably impact the person's own development.

This is why personal development is seen not only as an individual process, but also as a contribution to the well-being of the family, society, nature and future generations.

The Mission of Space Psychology#

The mission of Cosmic Psychology is to create a holistic model of the human being that helps integrate scientific knowledge, philosophical understanding, and cultural heritage to achieve a deeper understanding of human nature, improve health, develop creative personalities, and foster harmonious relationships between humans, society, nature, and the Universe.

The Connection between New Psychology DREVO and Cosmic Psychology#

Cosmic psychologyis a natural continuation and the next stage of developmentNew Psychology DREVOIt relies entirely on its fundamental principles, preserving the concept of man as a single, multidimensional, and constantly evolving living system.

DREVO's new psychology examines the human being through the interconnectedness of their body, psyche, consciousness, will, heredity, values, social environment, culture, and spiritual development. Its primary goal is to understand the internal patterns of personality formation, the mechanisms of mental health, and the laws of harmonious human development.

Cosmic psychology maintains this foundation while expanding the boundaries of research. It views humans not only as part of family, clan, society, and the Earth's biosphere, but also as an element of a broader system of natural and cosmic processes.

Her research interests include:

  • the influence of natural cycles and ecosystems on humans;
  • the impact of geophysical and astronomical factors;
  • the role of collective consciousness, culture and information environment;
  • human interaction with nature, society and technology;
  • formation of worldview and values;
  • philosophical questions of man's place in the Universe and his responsibility to future generations.

Cosmic psychology seeks to integrate knowledge from various scientific and humanistic disciplines, including psychology, medicine, biology, genetics, neuroscience, anthropology, ecology, sociology, astronomy, philosophy, and cultural studies, while maintaining a clear distinction between scientific knowledge, philosophical understanding, and spiritual and cultural traditions.

As part of its philosophical research, Cosmic Psychology also studies the ideas of various civilizations about the soul, consciousness, vital energy, aura, human field interactions, and other concepts, considering them as part of the world's cultural heritage and a subject of philosophical analysis, rather than as scientifically established facts.

Thus, New Psychology of DREVOis the foundation on which the understanding of the inner nature of man is built, whereasCosmic psychologyexpands this model by including man in the broader context of family, clan, society, nature, planet and the Universe.

They can be represented as successive levels of a single system of knowledge:

Cosmic Faith DREVOanswers the question:
"What is man's place in the Universe and what values ​​determine his path?"

New Psychology of DREVOanswers the question:
"How is a person structured and by what laws does his inner world develop?"

Cosmic psychologyanswers the question:
“How does a person interact with family, clan, society, nature, the planet and the Universe, and how do these relationships influence his development?”

Together, these three areas form a unified worldview system, DREVO, which unites philosophy, psychology, scientific knowledge, cultural heritage, and human responsibility to present and future generations.

Cosmic Psychology: the influence of external factors on a person, identifying talents and supporting the individual#

One of the main areas of Cosmic Psychology is the study of the influence of the external environment on the formation of personality, health, the development of abilities and the realization of a person's life potential.

Cosmic psychology proceeds from the premise that personality development is determined not only by innate characteristics, but also by the complex interactions of heredity, family, society, nature, and the environment. Therefore, humans are viewed as open living systems, continuously interacting with the outside world.

Influence of external factors#

Cosmic psychology studies the influence of various factors on a person, including:

Biological factors#

heredity;

features of intrauterine development;

age-related changes;

hormonal processes;

nutrition;

microbiome;

physical activity;

quality of sleep;

general health condition.

Family and Kin#

Studied:

  • pedigree;
  • family history;
  • models of education;
  • relationships between generations;
  • transmission of knowledge, values ​​and traditions;
  • the influence of family environment on personality formation.

Social environment#

The influence of:

  • education;
  • professional activities;
  • environment;
  • culture;
  • language;
  • mass media;
  • digital environment;
  • social networks;
  • collective processes and social institutions.

Natural environment#

The following are being considered:

  • climate;
  • change of seasons;
  • air and water quality;
  • natural landscapes;
  • noise level;
  • illumination;
  • biological diversity;
  • ecological state of the territory of residence.

Geophysical and astronomical factors#

The subject of the research may be:

  • change of day and night;
  • seasonal rhythms;
  • solar activity;
  • geomagnetic environment;
  • atmospheric processes;
  • lunar cycles;
  • other natural and astronomical phenomena.

Space psychology views these factors as possible environmental elements, whose influence is studied using scientific methods where possible. If certain hypothetical effects lack convincing scientific evidence, they are considered research hypotheses rather than established facts.

Identifying talents and abilities#

One of the most important tasks of Cosmic Psychology is the timely identification of a person’s natural abilities.

Each person possesses a unique combination of intellectual, emotional, creative, physical, and social qualities. The goal of the study is not to compare people with each other, but to find the most natural direction for each personality's development.

A comprehensive assessment may include:

  • analysis of interests and sustainable motivation;
  • observation of the peculiarities of thinking;
  • study of memory, attention and learning;
  • assessment of communication skills;
  • revealing creative potential;
  • analysis of leadership qualities;
  • research of practical skills;
  • assessment of emotional stability;
  • study of physical potential;
  • professional and educational counseling.
  • In case of voluntary consent of a person, the following may also be taken into account:
  • family history;
  • health condition;
  • medical examinations;
  • results of psychological diagnostics;
  • lifestyle features.

At the individual's discretion, cultural and symbolic practices of self-knowledge, such as an astrological birth chart, iridological traditions, or palm analysis, may be incorporated into the overall picture. These are considered solely as complementary elements of a personal worldview and are not used as standalone methods of diagnosis or life-changing decision-making.

Individual development support#

After identifying strengths and areas of development, Cosmic Psychology involves supporting a person at various stages of his life.

Such support may include:

  • assistance in choosing education;
  • vocational guidance;
  • development of talents;
  • support for creative projects;
  • psychological counseling;
  • lifestyle recommendations;
  • development of leadership qualities;
  • assistance in building family relationships;
  • teaching;
  • participation in educational, cultural and social projects.
  • The main goal of support is to help a person realize their own potential without imposing a predetermined life path.

The principle of individuality#

Cosmic psychology proceeds from the assumption that each person is unique.

There are no two identical life paths or universal models of success. Therefore, personal development must take into account a person's innate characteristics, life experiences, health, cultural environment, interests, abilities, and personal life goals.

The main goal#

The main goal of Cosmic Psychology is to create conditions in which each person can fully develop their abilities, maintain physical and mental health, find their place in society, and realize themselves through activities that benefit family, community, nature, and future generations.

Talent is not seen as a privilege reserved for a few, but as a unique combination of abilities that each person possesses. The goal of Cosmic Psychology is to help individuals discover these abilities, develop them, and apply them for the benefit of their own lives and the world around them.

Cosmic Psychology: The Study of the Influence of Astronomical and Astrological Factors#

One of the areas of Cosmic Psychology is the study of the possible influence of natural, astronomical and culturally determined astrological factors on the mental state, behavior, well-being and personal development.

Cosmic psychology assumes that humans are part of the Earth's natural environment and are constantly influenced by various external processes. Some of these processes have been well studied by modern science, while others remain the subject of ongoing research or relate to philosophical and cultural traditions.

Astronomical and natural factors#

Space psychology studies the influence of factors for which physical mechanisms of action exist or scientific research is being conducted, including:

  • change of day and night;
  • seasonal cycles;
  • duration of daylight hours;
  • solar activity;
  • geomagnetic disturbances;
  • atmospheric processes;
  • climate change;
  • lunar cycles and associated natural rhythms;
  • other astronomical and geophysical phenomena.

The study of these factors is based on data from astronomy, geophysics, chronobiology, ecology, medicine, psychology and other scientific disciplines.

Astrological traditions#

A separate area is the study of astrology as a historical, cultural and philosophical phenomenon.

Cosmic psychology explores:

  • the history of the emergence of various astrological systems;
  • their influence on the worldview and culture of different peoples;
  • psychological meaning of symbols and archetypes;
  • the use of astrological models in the processes of self-knowledge;
  • a person's subjective perception of his own personality through symbolic systems.

If a person voluntarily wishes, his astrological birth chart can be considered as one of the elements of a personal worldview analysis or a cultural tradition of self-knowledge.

At the same time, Cosmic Psychology does not consider astrology as a scientifically proven method of diagnosing personality, predicting events, or making medical, psychological, legal, or other important decisions.

Scientific and philosophical approach#

Cosmic psychology adheres to the principle of distinguishing between levels of knowledge.

Scientifically proven information about astronomical, geophysical and biological processes is used as a basis for research.

Astrological concepts are considered part of the world's cultural heritage, philosophy, and symbolic systems of self-knowledge. They can be the subject of psychological and cultural studies, but are not considered established scientific facts without corresponding empirical evidence.

Purpose of the research#

The aim of this direction is to study how the external environment – ​​natural, astronomical, cultural and informational – can influence a person’s condition, their adaptation, lifestyle, self-perception, worldview and personal development.

Research is conducted in compliance with the principles of voluntariness, critical thinking, scientific integrity, respect for cultural diversity, and a constant willingness to revise conclusions as new verifiable data becomes available.
