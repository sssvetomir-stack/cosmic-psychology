Feelings

FeelingsFeelings are internal experiences that arise in response to events, thoughts, interactions, and situations. They reflect our perception of the world, our values, beliefs, and emotional state. Feelings are an essential part of human nature, connecting us to ourselves, others, and the world around us.

What are feelings?#

Emotional reaction

Feelings are responses to external or internal stimuli. They help us interpret what's happening and understand our connection to events.

Language of the Soul

Feelings convey what is difficult to express in words. They are the Soul's way of communicating with us, pointing out our needs and desires.

Mindfulness tool

Feelings help us better understand ourselves: what makes us happy, what worries us, what inspires us, or what requires change.

A bridge to other people

Through feelings we establish connections, express love, compassion, joy, share grief and support each other.

Basic types of feelings#

Positive

Joy, love, gratitude, inspiration, peace.

These feelings fill life with light, give energy and promote development.

Negative

Sadness, fear, anger, envy, guilt.

They signal problems, needs or conflicts that require attention and work on oneself.

Complicated feelings

Ambivalence, longing, regret, confusion.

These feelings often arise in difficult situations and require deep reflection.

Feelings in the context of the TREE#

Awareness through feelings

In the TREE practice, feelings are perceived as an important tool for understanding oneself and the world. They help participants better understand their needs and find harmony.

Expression of feelings

Through art, theatre, music, and shared circles, DREVO participants can safely express their feelings, share them with others, and receive support.

Collective empathy

Respect and acceptance of each other's feelings creates an atmosphere of trust and unity in the community.

Feelings and nature

Interacting with nature helps calm negative feelings and fill the inner world with light and harmony.

How to work with feelings?#

Acceptance

Don't ignore your feelings or suppress them. Accept them as a natural part of your life.

Awareness

Try to understand where the feeling comes from: what caused it, what is it trying to say?

Expression

Find healthy ways to express your feelings: through words, creativity, movement, or meditation.

Balanced perception

Accept positive feelings with gratitude, and perceive negative ones as an opportunity for growth.

Empathy for others

Respect the feelings of others, even if they differ from yours.

The role of feelings in life#

Wayfinder

Feelings help us understand what is important and what is not, where to move and what changes are needed.

Energy source

Strong feelings, such as love or inspiration, give strength for creativity, work and self-realization.

Connection with other people

Feelings help to establish deep and sincere relationships based on understanding and support.

Harmony with yourself

Consciously working with feelings helps us find balance, inner peace, and a fuller life.

Feelings— are the foundation through which a person perceives, understands, and expresses themselves and the world around them. They can be both a challenge and a gift, helping us grow, love, and find meaning.

What feelings are most important to you? And how do you interact with them?
