Harmony

Harmony— is a state of balance, harmony, and unity. It's not just the absence of conflict, but a deep sense of integrity and order that permeates a person's inner world and their interactions with the environment. In the contextTREE, harmony becomes a fundamental principle uniting all areas: health, culture, cohabitation, technology and nature.

What is Harmony?#

Harmony as balance

It is a state in which different parts of a system (be it body, mind, society or nature) are in balance, complementing and supporting each other.

Harmony as agreement

The interaction of parts, based on mutual respect and understanding, creates a sense of order and beauty.

Harmony as inner peace

Within a person, harmony manifests itself as a feeling of calm, clarity and confidence.

Harmony as a connection with nature

It is an awareness of one's place in the world, respect for natural cycles and a desire to live in harmony with the environment.

Harmony as creativity

Creating something beautiful, when every detail finds its place, creates a harmonious result.

Harmony in the TREE#

Harmony in body and spirit

Through health practices such as gymnastics, breathing exercises, and meditation, DREVO participants learn to find balance between the physical and spiritual.

Harmony in the community

Interactions within TREE are built on respect, support, and a commitment to shared goals. This creates a space for deep communication and collaboration.

Harmony with nature

An ecological approach, respect for the earth, and the rational use of resources are ways of expressing harmony in actions.

Harmony in creativity

Art, poetry, music, and theatre help us express our inner harmony and share it with others.

Harmony of technology and nature

At DREVO, technology is used to maintain the natural balance, not destroy it. It's an example of combining innovation with respect for the environment.

How to achieve Harmony?#

Through awareness

Observe your thoughts, actions, and state. Harmony begins with self-understanding.

Through health care

Balance between body, mind and spirit helps you find inner peace.

Through respect for nature

Be mindful of how you interact with the environment. Find ways to live more sustainably.

Through relationships

Build communication based on respect, empathy and mutual support.

Through creativity

Express yourself through art or creating something useful, finding joy in the process.

Principles of Harmony in the TREE#

Integrity: Each part of the system is important and must be connected to other parts.

Natural rhythm: Respect for the cycles of life, whether in nature, work or leisure.

Balance: Searching for moderation in everything – in actions, words, consumption.

Mutual supportHarmony is achieved when everyone helps each other without forgetting about themselves.

Beauty and simplicityHarmony is born in simplicity and naturalness, without unnecessary complication.

HarmonyTREE is not only a goal, but also a way of life. It's a state that allows everyone to feel part of a larger and more beautiful world, to act in harmony with themselves and others.

How do you experience harmony in your life? Where do you feel it most?
