Justice

Justice— is a principle based on fairness, equality, and respect that governs relationships between people, society, and nature. It is a fundamental basis for harmonious existence, helping to establish a balance between individual rights, responsibilities, and the common good. In the contextTREE, justice is a key value that is manifested in the organization of the community, the interaction of participants and respect for nature.

What is justice?#

Justice as balance

It is a state in which each party receives what is due to them according to their contribution, needs and rights.

Justice as respect for the dignity of everyone

It is a recognition of the uniqueness and value of every person, regardless of their status, origin or capabilities.

Justice as honesty

The principle that actions and decisions are made on the basis of truth, without bias or lies.

Justice as responsibility

It is a willingness to consider the consequences of one's actions on others and on nature, making decisions with respect for the common good.

Justice in the TREE#

Justice in the community

Each participant has the right to vote and the opportunity to contribute to the common cause.

Decisions are made on the basis of dialogue, consensus and consideration of the needs of all.

Environmental justice

Respect for nature as an equal part of the system: wise use of resources, protection of ecosystems and support for sustainable development.

Fairness in Economics

The principles of a fair economy in DREVO include equal access to goods, support for those in need, and transparency in the distribution of resources.

Fairness in relationships

Building relationships based on trust, honesty and respect.

Justice for future generations

In TREE, decisions are made with long-term consequences in mind in order to preserve resources and harmony for children and future generations.

How to achieve justice?#

Awareness and honesty

Learn to analyze your actions by asking questions: is this fair for me, others, and the world around me?

Respect for the rights of others

Recognize the value of other people's opinions, needs, and desires, even if they differ from yours.

Transparency

Decision-making and action must be open and clear to all participants.

Responsibility for the consequences

Justice requires taking into account how your actions affect others and nature.

Supporting equality

Creating conditions where every person has access to opportunities, resources and respect.

Principles of Justice in TREE#

Equality of opportunity

Every participant has a chance to express themselves, contribute and be heard.

Mutual support

Those who have more resources help those who need them.

Balance between individual and collective

Justice is manifested when personal interests do not dominate the common good, but are also not suppressed by the collective.

Saving and restoring

All actions are aimed at preserving natural and human resources, as well as restoring balance where it has been disturbed.

Ethics of action

Every action is considered in terms of its integrity, usefulness, and impact on others.

Justice as a path to harmony#

Justice helps resolve conflicts, build trust, and maintain harmony in society. It requires awareness, effort, and the ability to see the world from different perspectives.
